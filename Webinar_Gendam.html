Questions

Before you register.

Is the masterclass really free?

Yes. There's no charge to register or attend.

How will I get the joining link?

After you register, you'll receive the Zoom link and reminders on WhatsApp and email. Please check both, including your spam folder.

Will there be a recording?

This is a live, experiential session and the practice works best live, so please plan to attend in real time.

Is this hypnosis or mind control?

No. Ancient Magnetism focuses on your own state, attention and presence. Manipulating or controlling others is explicitly outside its purpose.

Do I need any prior experience?

It's designed for people who already have some healing, coaching or spiritual practice, but curious beginners are welcome.

Will anything be sold?

Towards the end, Rohan will share how you can continue working with him if you'd like to. There's no obligation — the masterclass is complete on its own.

Can I join from my phone?

Yes. A laptop gives the best experience, but a phone with Zoom and earphones works fine.
